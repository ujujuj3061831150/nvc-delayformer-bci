\smallskip
\noindent\textbf{Biomedical Context \& Problem Statement:}
Hybrid electroencephalography (EEG) and functional near-infrared spectroscopy (fNIRS) non-invasively probe complementary electrophysiological transients and localized hemodynamic dynamics. However, translating hybrid systems to zero-calibration cross-subject clinical BCIs faces a fundamental biophysical bottleneck: disparate temporal scales (milliseconds vs.\ 3.5--6.5\,s). Unconstrained neural attention models exploit spurious temporal alignments in leave-one-subject-out (LOSO) regimes, impairing generalization across unseen individuals.

\smallskip
\noindent\textbf{Key Contributions \& Empirical Findings:}
We present \textbf{NVC-DelayFormer}, an end-to-end biophysically constrained architecture coupling electrophysiological and optical streams through explicit neurovascular coupling inductive priors:
\begin{enumerate}\setlength\itemsep{0.15em}
  \item \textbf{Biophysically Constrained Cross-Attention:} Multi-head cross-attention is regularized by continuous Gaussian hemodynamic delay priors ($\tau_h \in [3.5, 6.5]$\,s) and strict token timestamp causal masking ($t_{\mathrm{nirs}} \ge t_{\mathrm{eeg}}$), eliminating acausal optimization shortcuts.
  \item \textbf{Uncertainty-Aware Entropy-Guided Dual-Stream Gating:} An adaptive bottleneck network dynamically balances deep cross-attention tokens against shallow biophysical spectral summaries conditioned on predictive distribution entropy.
  \item \textbf{Causal Online Adaptation \& Multi-Task Generalization:} We introduce Causal Recursive Euclidean Alignment (CrEA), stabilizing covariance within 5--8 trials under empirical Bayes shrinkage. Across 29 participants on the TU Berlin benchmark under LOSO cross-validation, NVC-DelayFormer achieves a 3-seed grand mean accuracy of \textbf{78.22\% $\pm$ 1.00\%} (primary seed: \textbf{79.37\% $\pm$ 7.73\%}), significantly outperforming 2D-EEGNet ($p = 1.10\times 10^{-4}$), covariance-aligned Shrinkage-LDA ($p = 0.0167$), and unconstrained cross-attention ($p = 7.18\times 10^{-4}$). External validations across 3,612 held-out trials demonstrate robust generalizability on $n$-back working memory (73.24\%) and motor imagery (66.32\%).
  \item \textbf{Bedside-Ready Edge Profile:} The architecture operates with only \textbf{63,932 trainable parameters} and \textbf{24.79\,M MACs}, executing in \textbf{2.22\,ms} on a laptop GPU (P99: 4.48\,ms), ready for real-time bedside closed-loop deployment.
\end{enumerate}
