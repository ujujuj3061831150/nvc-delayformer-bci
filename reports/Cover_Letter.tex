\smallskip
\noindent\textbf{Biomedical Context \& Clinical Rationale:}
Hybrid EEG--fNIRS non-invasively captures complementary electrophysiological transients and localized capillary hemodynamics. Translating hybrid systems to zero-calibration clinical BCIs (e.g., for motor-impaired or locked-in individuals unable to undergo tedious calibrations) faces a fundamental biophysical bottleneck: disparate biological timescales (milliseconds vs.\ 3.5--6.5\,s). Standard unconstrained cross-attention architectures exploit spurious temporal coincidences in leave-one-subject-out (LOSO) regimes, degrading generalization across unseen individuals.

\smallskip
\noindent\textbf{Key Contributions \& Empirical Rigor:}
We present \textbf{NVC-DelayFormer}, an end-to-end biophysically constrained architecture coupling electrophysiological and optical feature streams through explicit neurovascular coupling inductive priors:
\begin{enumerate}\setlength\itemsep{0.08em}\setlength\parskip{0pt}
  \item \textbf{Biophysically Constrained Cross-Attention:} Multi-head attention is regularized by continuous Gaussian hemodynamic delay kernels ($\tau_h \in [3.5, 6.5]$\,s) and strict token timestamp causal masking ($t_{\mathrm{nirs}} \ge t_{\mathrm{eeg}}$), eliminating acausal optimization shortcuts.
  \item \textbf{Entropy-Guided Dual-Stream Gating:} An adaptive bottleneck dynamically balances deep cross-modal tokens against shallow biophysical spectral summaries conditioned on predictive distribution entropy, safeguarded by auxiliary waveform reconstruction.
  \item \textbf{Causal Online Adaptation \& Multi-Task Generalization:} We introduce Causal Recursive Euclidean Alignment (CrEA), eliminating session lookahead and stabilizing reference covariances within 5--8 trials under empirical Bayes shrinkage (retaining 94.9\% of offline performance in trial-causal streaming evaluation). Across 29 participants on the TU Berlin benchmark under LOSO cross-validation, NVC-DelayFormer achieves a 3-seed grand mean accuracy of \textbf{78.22\% $\pm$ 1.00\%} (primary seed: \textbf{79.37\% $\pm$ 7.73\%}), significantly outperforming standard 2D-EEGNet ($p = 1.10\times 10^{-4}$), covariance-aligned Shrinkage-LDA ($p = 0.0167$), and unconstrained cross-attention ($p = 7.18\times 10^{-4}$). External validations across 3,612 held-out trials demonstrate robust generalizability on $n$-back working memory (73.24\%) and motor imagery (66.32\%).
  \item \textbf{Bedside-Ready Edge Profile:} The architecture operates with only \textbf{63,932 parameters} and \textbf{24.79\,M MACs}, executing in \textbf{2.22\,ms} on a laptop GPU (P99: 4.48\,ms, >400\,Hz throughput), ready for real-time bedside closed-loop deployment.
\end{enumerate}
